# My Favorite Series: Arcane

## About the show
Arcane is an animated series on Netflix based on the game League of Legends. It's set in two cities, the rich city of Piltover and the underground city of Zaun. The story follows two sisters, Vi and Jinx, and how the conflict between the two cities pulls them apart. The *animation* is one of the best I've seen.

> Vi will always protect her sister, no matter what.
